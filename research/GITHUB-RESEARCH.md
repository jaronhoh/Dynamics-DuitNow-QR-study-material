1. Which fields are defined by PayNet?
2. Which fields are acquirer-specific?
3. Which fields are static?
4. Which fields change for every Dynamic QR?
5. How are amount and reference represented?
6. What is the role of 82?
7. What information is required from the acquirer?
8. What can be generated independently?
9. What requires merchant onboarding or backend validation?
10. Why can a structurally valid QR still be rejected by a bank?

## 4. Critical Lesson

Do not copy a GitHub QR generator and assume it produces a production-valid Merchant Dynamic QR.

Structurally valid QR is not equal to acquirer-processable QR.

## 7. What We Must Not Do

- Do not treat a P2P Receive QR as a Merchant QR.
- Do not change 52 and assume the product type changed.
- Do not copy an 82 value between different QR instances.
- Do not assume CRC correctness means bank acceptance.
- Do not assume scanner display means payment authorization.
- Do not implement the production generator before obtaining a genuine Merchant Dynamic QR baseline.

## 8. Highest-Value Missing Evidence

A genuine RHB Reflex Merchant Presented Dynamic QR generated by the official merchant system.

Target controlled samples:
- RM0.01 / INV-001
- RM0.02 / INV-002
- RM1.00 / INV-003
- RM1.00 / INV-004

For each sample record raw QR payload, decoded TLV, screenshot, amount, reference, generation time, 26 Merchant Account Information, 52, 54, 62, 82, 63, scanner result and payment result.

## 9. Evidence Classification

- Official Specification
- Official Sample
- GitHub Research
- Our Experiment
- Inference

GitHub research and our inference must not be presented as official PayNet behavior unless independently confirmed.

## 10. Final Research Principle

The project is trying to understand how a legitimate Merchant Presented Dynamic DuitNow QR is generated and accepted, not merely how to construct a QR-looking string.

The final implementation should be based on PayNet specification + official merchant/acquirer sample + controlled comparison + successful bank-side validation + documented implementation constraints.
